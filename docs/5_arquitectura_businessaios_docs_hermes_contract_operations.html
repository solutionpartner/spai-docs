Hermes / SP Supervisor — Contract Operations Policy

Hermes may reason about contract state but must not become a signer or trust-service provider.

Allowed by default

  • Read legal gate status and missing requirements.
  • Read contract metadata, evidence verification and provider state.
  • Explain which document blocks onboarding.
  • Prepare a draft envelope/request for human review.
  • Recommend operational remediation when a webhook/provider request fails.

Approval-gated

  • Registering a new legal template version.
  • Sending a contract/signature request.
  • Voiding/cancelling a pending signature request.
  • Changing the required-document matrix for a customer/plan.
  • Importing historical contract evidence.

These actions should use the Phase 14 Supervisor Action Gateway rather than direct database mutation.

Never delegated to Hermes

  • Signing/accepting a contract on behalf of a customer or SolutionPartner representative.
  • Claiming that a person has authority to bind a legal entity without verified business process evidence.
  • Altering a published template version or signed document.
  • Fabricating provider evidence, timestamps, signatures or identity-verification results.
  • Downgrading a signature level solely to make onboarding pass.

Production design target: Hermes sees status and policy, while regulated signing remains between the authorised human signer and the external trust/signature provider.
