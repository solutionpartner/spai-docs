Phase 21 - Production Acceptance

A Phase 21 environment can be marked governance-ready when:

  • Every production-facing managed capability has a service record, owner and criticality.
  • Critical/high services have at least one meaningful SLO or explicit documented exception.
  • Monitoring incidents can be linked to services.
  • Problem records can correlate multiple incidents and retain root cause/workaround.
  • Change records enforce lifecycle transitions and retain approval identity.
  • Release/deployment remains the execution source of truth; change records only govern and reference it.
  • Service requests, CIs, knowledge and improvement records are tenant-scoped where applicable.
  • Hermes has read-only service-management visibility and cannot self-approve changes.
  • RBAC includes a service-manager role and read-only scopes for observers/auditors.
  • Security audit records service-governance mutations.
  • Full regression test suite passes.

Commercial acceptance wording must not imply ITIL/PeopleCert certification, accreditation or endorsement without separate verified rights.
