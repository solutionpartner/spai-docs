Rollback runbook

  1. Identify the completed deployment that introduced the problematic version.
  2. Confirm an active signed release manifest exists for its from_version.
  3. Call POST /deployments/{deployment_id}/rollback with the requesting operator.
  4. Review the generated deployment. It must be pending_approval.
  5. A human/admin approves the rollback deployment.
  6. The client worker claims and executes it through the same signed pull channel.
  7. Confirm tenant health and component version after completion.

A rollback is deliberately not an emergency shell command. For infrastructure emergencies that require direct VPS access, use a separate break-glass operational process outside the AI/Control Plane path.
